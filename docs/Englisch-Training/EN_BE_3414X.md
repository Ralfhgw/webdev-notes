Das Dokument bietet eine Anleitung, wie man im Englischsprechenden Geschäftskontext Gegensatz und Opposition mithilfe verschiedener Connectors (Verbindungswörter) ausdrücken kann. Ziel ist es, klare und elegante Kontraste zwischen Meinungen, Tätigkeiten oder Situationen zu formulieren.

#### Wichtige Wörter und Phrasen sind unter anderem:
    - but
    - however
    - nevertheless
    - despite this
    - on the one hand / on the other hand
    - in spite of this
    - even so
    - though

#### Beispiele aus dem Dokument:
    1. "I was quite lazy at work today! I finished everything I had to do, though."
    2. "On the one hand, investing in a start-up is risky. However, it can also be extremely profitable, as we’ve seen with Uber and Airbnb."
    3. "He missed his train this morning. Despite this, he got to the office on time."
    4. "They tried to whisper. I could still hear them, though."

#### Strukturierte Sätze mit Kontrast:
    - "My team isn’t very… Despite this, they…"
    - "Some people think my job is… though…"

#### Eigene zusätzliche Beispiele:
    - "The new policy has some advantages. Nevertheless, it also causes some inconvenience."
    - "She enjoys working remotely. On the other hand, her colleagues prefer face-to-face meetings."
    - "The project was challenging. In spite of this, we managed to complete it ahead of schedule."
    - "Our sales increased last quarter. Even so, we need to focus on improving customer service."
