Das Dokument ist ein Lernleitfaden, dessen goals es sind, dass der Lernende eine Vielfalt technischer terms zum Beschriften und Beschreiben eines graph erkennen und abrufen kann, und ein graph oder komplexere statistics genau beschreiben kann, um einen Standpunkt zu untermauern.

Der Leitfaden bietet verschiedene Phrasen, um Grafiken zu beschreiben:
#### Describing the topic (Beschreibung des Themas):
- Phrases shown: The subject/topic of the graph is…; The diagram provides information on…; These statistics indicate…; The chart illustrates how….

#### Verbs to describe graphs (Verben zur Beschreibung von Grafiken):
Verbs and example sentences shown:
- **depict:** The chart depicts the changes in the market.
- **suggest:** The graph suggests that sales have remained stable.
- **imply:** The figures imply that there has been no real change.
- **conclude:** We can conclude that sales figures will remain the same for the foreseeable future.
- **tell:** The pie chart tells us that sales of fiction and non-fiction are equally divided..

#### Giving facts about the data (Angabe von Fakten über die Daten):
- **Phrases shown:** The chart was published by…; The data is from the year…; The data is taken from the following source:…; The figures are given in per cent.; The table specifies…; This value is given in the form of a curve.; This value is displayed as a circle.; The data was collected by….

#### Reading the graph (Interpretation der Grafik/Daten):
- **Phrases shown:** The graph shows the upward/downward trend of…; The table shows the rising/declining development of…; What the chart shows is a positive/negative trend…; If you compare data point A and data point B…; You can notice great similarities between….

Das Dokument enthält auch verschiedene Schreib- und Sprechaufgaben, die die Anwendung dieser phrases und verbs erfordern, wie zum Beispiel die Beschreibung eines graph, eines line chart oder die Auswahl des passendsten chart zur Darstellung bestimmter data.

#### Zusätzliche Beispiele für die Verwendung der Verben:
- **Illustrate:** The bar chart illustrates the significant difference in expenditure between Q1 and Q2.
- **Reflect:** The slight drop in November reflects the seasonal downturn in the industry.
- **Show:** The figures show a sharp increase in production over the last quarter.
- **Indicate:** The line graph indicates a period of stability followed by a sudden fluctuation.
